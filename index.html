Sproutle Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · Sproutle is made by Learning Forever LLC

Sproutle is a virtual pet that grows from your steps and sleep. This page explains what the app reads, what leaves your phone, and how to delete it.

Apple Health

With your permission, Sproutle reads your step count and sleep from Apple Health. It never writes to Apple Health.

  • Your Health data is used only to feed and rest your pet, and to show your own stats.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, never sold and never shared with anyone except as described under Friends below.
  • Sleep never leaves your phone.

What is stored on our server

Sproutle uses Supabase to host its server. It stores:

  • An account ID, made automatically when you first use Friends. It is anonymous unless you choose to add an email.
  • Your email address, only if you choose to save your account with it. It is used to send sign-in codes, nothing else.
  • Your friend code, your pet's name and what friends see: your pet's look, mood, stage and bars, and today's activity level.
  • Your step counts (today and your 7-day average), only while "Compare steps with friends" is on in Settings.
  • A private backup of your pet (its bars, food, petals, purchases and recent daily steps), so you can bring it back on a new phone. Only you can read it.
  • Your friend list and gifts you send or receive.

Friends

People you accept as friends can see your pet, its name, mood and today's activity level, plus your step counts if you allow it. They never see your sleep.

No ads, no tracking

Sproutle shows no ads, uses no ad networks and does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell any data.

Emails you send us

"Send a note to the developer" opens your own Mail app. If you send it, we receive your note, the app version and your pet's name, stage and color.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete my account. Seven days later your account, friend list, gifts and pet backup are permanently removed from our server, and the pet on your phone is erased. You can cancel any time before then. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone right away.

Children

Sproutle is meant for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use Settings → Send a note to the developer in the app.
